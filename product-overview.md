# Product Overview

## Product Name
AI Health Insights

## Description
AI Health Insights is a digital health companion that uses a multimodal AI model to provide conversational health information. The app helps users understand potential cuases for symptoms, identifies health risks, and also offers preventative care advice. Unlike standard search engines, it is specificlaly trained on credible health research and physician insights to provide a safer, more nuanced bridge between initial symptoms and professional medical help. 

## Target Users
This product is targeted towsards inviduasl who are unsure if their symptoms require a doctor's visit or are intimidated by the clinical environment. It's also targeted towards those without a primary physician, those worried about the high costs of unneecessary medical care, young people who may want to explore health concerns privately before sharing them with guardias, and individuals without a current primary care provider looking for guided health navigation. 

## Problem Being Solved
This app mainly addresses the panic and anxiety that arises from frantic internet searches that often lead to worst-case scenarios and misinformation. It solves the lack of context in static health articles by offering an interactive dialouge. Additionally, it also lowers the barrier to care for those who face high costs or long wait times for remote clinical consultations, providing immediate, credible information that prepares users for actual appointments rather than replacing them. 

## Value Proposition
Users would choose this product becasue AI Health Insights allows users to transition from stressed to prepared about their healthcare. This is because of how the app provides a specialized AI that is able to contextualize symptoms rather than just listing worst-case diagnoses. Users are also able to take features like the physician reviews and medical summaries to their appointments to reduce intimidation. Also, reccomendations for nearby doctors based on insurance and specific filters (gender, experience) to streamline a user's path to getting cae. Lastly, the app's enhanced security with the ability to delete chat histories and keep sensitive data anonymous all serve as reasons why users would choose this product.
